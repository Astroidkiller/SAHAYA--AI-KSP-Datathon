## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording

1. **Screen Resolution**: Set recording software (e.g. OBS, Loom, PowerPoint) to **1080p (1920x1080)**.
2. **Mouse Movement**: Move the cursor smoothly without fast erratic clicks.
3. **Pacing**: Speak at a steady, clear pace (~130 words per minute).
